## Publishing rules
- Use the permanent Deal of the Day page: https://buybetterfinds.com/deal-of-the-day/
- Every published Amazon link must go directly to the exact product detail page and include `tag=buybetterfi06-20`. Never use Amazon search-result links.
- Confirm current price, reference/list price when shown, discount, stock, product rating, review count, exact ASIN, and working product image on publishing day.
- Do not describe a discount as “deep” or “best price” without evidence. Prices and availability can change.
- Keep the product's image consistent with the exact product being linked.
- Keep Buy Better Finds Pick separate from Deal of the Day.
- Rotate across categories; avoid repeating recently featured items such as the Anker Nano Power Bank, Apple AirPods, Owala FreeSip, and Seesii Electric Car Jack unless there is a compelling new deal.
- If a candidate fails verification, replace it with another qualifying product from the same category. Do not publish a broken image or a generic search link.
