## How to make a budget gift look more special

- **Keep the presentation simple.** Use one consistent wrapping color and a small ribbon or gift tag.
- **Group small items thoughtfully.** Pair a coffee accessory with a favorite treat, or a self-care item with a handwritten note.
- **Check the details before checkout.** Confirm the variant, pack size, delivery estimate, return terms, and current price.
- **Choose for the person, not just the price.** A useful item that fits someone's routine will feel more thoughtful than a random bargain.

## The Buy Better takeaway

A good under-$25 gift should feel intentional, not like a compromise. Pick the item that fits the recipient's interests, verify the current listing, and leave room in the budget for wrapping or a small personal touch.
